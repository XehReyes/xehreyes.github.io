Bio

I am a Colombian scholar and artist working at the intersection of narrative, immersion, and learning. I hold a double Ph.D. in Digital Humanities from the University of Genoa (Italy) and in Communication Sciences from Universidad del Norte (Colombia).

My research focuses on interactive digital narratives, immersive technologies, and film theory. I am a member of ARDIN, Virtual Networking Manager of CA18230 INDCOR (COST Action), Artist-in-Residence 2020–2021 at Schloss Solitude Akademie, and Artistic Research Fellow at Future Media Theaters Lab, University of Skövde (2022).

I am a member of the Colombian Academy of Cinematographic Arts. Moving between Europe and Colombia, working globally.
